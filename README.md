# HR-ANALYTICS-DASHBOARD-POWERBI
## Project Overview

Tool: Power BI   
Objective: Analyze employee attrition and identify workforce retention patterns  

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
-Total Employees: 1,470.
-Employee Attrition: 237.
-Attrition Rate: 16%.
-Average Age: 37 years.
-Average Salary: 7K.
-Average Years at Company: 7 years.

## Key Insights
-Age: Employees aged 26–35 have the highest attrition count (116).
-Salary: Employees earning below 5K account for 163 exits.
-Job Role: Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition count (62).
-Experience: Employees with one year at the company show 59 exits.
-Education: Life Sciences represents the largest education group among employee exits (38%).

## Business Recommendations
Improve compensation and benefits for lower-paid employees.
Strengthen onboarding and first-year employee support.
Investigate attrition among Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives.
Introduce career development and employee retention programs.

## Conclusion

The dashboard identifies employee groups with higher attrition counts, helping HR teams prioritize retention initiatives and improve workforce planning.
